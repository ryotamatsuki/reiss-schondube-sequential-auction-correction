## General candidate theorem (Q1)
Let [c,C] be the common cost support, 0<k<C-c, all four costs independent with common continuous density phi strictly positive on the interior. At the final auction select truthful effective-cost bids (firm 1 cost m if it did not win L, m+k if it won L; abstain above reserve), at every history. Firm 2 cost n in either case. Assume firm 2 first-round bid is l2, as in the source.

Given this strategy, firm 1 has unique interior threshold l1+h1(m), truncated at reserve or exit, where h1(m)=E[(M2-m)_+-(M2-m-k)_+], in [0,k]. This follows from first-round gain from winning at rival price x being x-l1-D1(m,M2); conditional on x, M2 independent. Sign change at l1+h1 globally determines optimum ignoring measure-zero ties. Reserve behavior against truthful firm 2 can be cap or abstention without changing payoff or interior rival bid distribution.

Take firm 2 type (l,n) with c+k<l<C and c<n<C. For any marginal bid x in (l,C), rival first cost x-h1(m) lies strictly in (c,C). Define D2(m,n)=(min(m+k,C)-n)_+-(m-n)_+. It is nonnegative and strictly positive for m in (n,C). For epsilon>0 sufficiently small with l+epsilon<C, the utility gain for changing b=l to b=l+epsilon is the exact switch-region integral
  Delta(epsilon)=integral_l^(l+epsilon) integral_c^C [D2(m,n)-(x-l)] phi(x-h1(m)) phi(m) dm dx.
At epsilon=0 the right derivative is integral_c^C D2(m,n) phi(l-h1(m)) phi(m) dm >0. Continuity yields a strict profitable deviation. The type set has positive probability and no altered tie or entry event. Thus no BNE with this final-stage selection and truthful first-round bidding by firm 2 exists under these hypotheses. This is a claim about the proposed equilibrium class, not all revised equilibria. No assertion on earlier sections' revenue equivalence.

Uniform normalization [0,1]: for l>k, rival bid density conditional on m is 1 on (l,1), so h2(n)=E[D2]=k(1-n)-(k-n)_+^2/2, and Delta(epsilon)=epsilon h2-epsilon^2/2. Choose epsilon=min(h2,1-l)/2, strictly positive. Exact Python/SymPy check (code/verify_general_rejection.py): 3420 rational-grid type/parameter cases; smallest checked gain 3/1280000; source example 53/200 raw units. Grid is a check, not proof or probability. Nested null k=0 gives D1=D2=0 and truthful bidding is again weakly dominant in both independent auctions. The predicted difference fails if surcharge is zero, or if relevant rival types cannot become less competitive (nonpositive conditional gap). Opposite/null checks must be retained.

## Stage-4 equilibrium contract (Q2)
Primary source-compatible completion A: two simultaneous sealed-bid second-price procurement rounds; all four costs independently drawn at t=0; each firm observes its own pair, others observe common primitives; after round 1 the winner/no-award status is public. Two bidders; cost m1+k only upon both awards to firm 1. Explicit exit action E, admissible bids [0,C), equal admitted bids split 1/2, only admitted bidder paid C, neither admitted means no award and no capacity consumption. The source treats C as exit at the proposed profile; both-exit/no-award and tie protocol are supplementary conventions for a fully specified revised game. Completion B sensitivity: allow actual bid C separately from exit, same tie and award rules. No claim that the source specified B. Costs [0,1] uniform for constructive solution; full positive-density class for disproof. Quasilinear risk-neutral payoffs, no entry cost or discount. Second-stage effective cost bidding selected as weakly dominant; equality at reserve and infeasible high costs handled explicitly. Bayes posterior on observed first-round public histories when defined; terminal dominant actions optimal for every posterior, including off-path. Target: Bayesian Nash equilibrium of induced round-1 game with sequentially rational truthful terminal continuation (PBE assessment with beliefs). Pure monotone in first cost as first solution class, justify/narrow and test mixed or nonmonotone alternative effects, no unique/global claim until proven. No unchecked solver failure treated as equilibrium. Full revised equilibrium under these conventions is not yet solved.

Write G_i^w,G_i^l,G_i^0 for terminal profit given first winner i, rival, none, evaluated from both effective costs and reserve. Let rival actual bid x or E. Global payoff gain relative to exiting first round:
  E[1{rival enters} q(b,x)(x-l_i-D_i) + 1{rival exits}(C-l_i+G_i^w-G_i^0)],
where q=1 if b<x, 1/2 if equal, else 0, and D_i=G_i^l-G_i^w. For firm 1, G1^0=G1^l; for firm 2, G2^0=G2^w. Exiting yields zero difference by definition. This separates the rival-exit atom from a legitimate reserve bid. Treat exact endpoint ties, no award, and deviations over all b or E. Interior FOCs in notes/01 are necessary only. Important boundary test for completion A: if firm 1 exits with positive probability, firm 2 with l2<C cannot optimally exit (b approaches C from below, gain tends to (C-l2)Pr(E1)>0); does not prove either firm actually exits or any equilibrium exists.
Solve conditional bid thresholds, cap/exit masses and all deviations; test whether payoff maximizer is attained at open boundary (possible nonexistence). Completion B permits an atom at C and tie payoff different from exit: solve separately if needed. Counterexamples to pure profiles are valid even while global equilibrium unresolved.
