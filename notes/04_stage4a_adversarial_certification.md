## T1 — exact theorem and finite-deviation proof
Independent common atomless private cost draws with continuous positive density phi on interior [c,C], c>=0, source 0<k<C-c. Each firm knows both own costs at start, sealed-bid second-price sequence and fair tie rule; firm1 incurs k only if awarded both; C reserve, one eligible bidder receives C. Choose effective-cost terminal bids and exit if above reserve (capped bid is equivalent a.s. when opponent bids below reserve), at every history. With this terminal selection, no BNE of induced first-stage game (and hence no PBE having this continuation) has firm2 bid its first cost a.s. This is a restricted nonexistence theorem for a strategy profile/class, not nonexistence of equilibrium in general.

Reconstruct terminal profits by realized allocation/payment. For firm1 with m, winning first rather than losing sacrifices D1=(n-m)_+-(n-m-k)_+ in terminal profit, for rival second cost n. For firm2 type n, winning first rather than losing sacrifices D2(m,n)=(min(m+k,C)-n)_+-(m-n)_+. All are nonnegative; D2=min(k,C-m)>0 when n<m<C. Effective-cost terminal bidding is weakly dominant for any posterior, so altered first-round histories do not invalidate continuation. At cost equality/reserve equality ties give zero to the marginal winner; measure-zero terminal boundaries irrelevant to the integrated deviation. Other terminal equilibria not excluded.

Assuming firm2 bids its first cost, rival first-round price X independent of rival second cost N. For firm1 own first cost L and second cost m, winning rather than losing gives conditional gain X-L-h1(m), h1(m)=E D1(m,N). This sign comparison over **all** admitted bids and exit gives unique interior best-reply threshold L+h1(m) (strictly positive price density); if threshold >=C, cap/exit is payoff equivalent under the maintained rival profile. Mixing cannot change interior bids, since every suboptimal action strictly loses relative to the maximizer. For m interior, 0<=h1(m)<=min(k,C-m). This is direct global optimality, not just an FOC.

Fix ANY firm2 interior type (l,n) with c<l<C, c<n<C. Choose c<m<C near C with m>n and C-m<l-c. Select a nondegenerate CLOSED interval J of such m, avoiding endpoints. D2>=d0=min(k,C-max J)>0 throughout J. Select e0>0 small, l+e0<C. For 0<e<=e0 and x in [l,l+e], x-h1(m) lies in a compact subinterval of (c,C) for m in J. Joint density of rival (interior bid x, second cost m) equals phi(x-h1(m))*phi(m) on this rectangle, bounded below by a positive constant. Therefore P(l<B1<l+e, M1 in J)>=K*e for some K>0. Interior B1 is atomless (L continuously distributed conditional on m); reserve/exit may be an atom at C but is outside the band. Thus P(l<B1<l+e)->0 as e->0. Raise bid from l to l+e. Direct payoff comparison on switch event gives
 Delta = E[1{l<B1<l+e}(D2-(B1-l))]
       >= d0*K*e - e*P(l<B1<l+e)>0 for sufficiently small e.
Outside band, first winner, payment, and selected continuation unchanged. Both bids feasible and strictly below C. All relevant first winner/rival winner/exit histories checked; ties in band probability zero; no reliance on both-exit rules. Countable rational e gives measurable positive-gain alternative on a positive-measure set (indeed all interior types conditional on best-reply rival strategy). No correlation assumption silently generalized. Covers Stage-4 domain l>c+k and stronger full interior own type domain; no need to enlarge paper scope beyond source parameters. This finite bound avoids differentiating a potentially nonuniform density near support boundaries.
